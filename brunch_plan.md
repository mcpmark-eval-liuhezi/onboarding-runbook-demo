## Menu

### 1. 手抓饼 (Hand-Pulled Pancake) — Main
**Difficulty:** ★★ (2/5) | **Category:** 早餐 (Breakfast) | **Servings:** 1 batch (scale up for 4)

**Ingredients (per batch):**
- All-purpose flour (普通面粉) — 200 g
- Boiling water (开水) — 100 ml
- Cold water (冷水) — 50 ml
- Cooking oil (食用油) — 15 ml
- Salt (盐) — 3 g
- Filling/filling ingredients: 1 egg, 30 g lettuce (生菜), 30 g ham (火腿), 1 slice of cheese (芝士片)

**Method:**
1. Mix the flour with the salt. Gradually pour in the boiling water while stirring, then add the cold water — this half-hot-water dough (半烫面) is what makes it flaky.
2. Knead into a smooth dough, rest it covered for ~30 minutes.
3. Divide into portions, roll each into a thin rectangle, brush with cooking oil, and sprinkle with salt.
4. Roll it up into a cylinder, then coil the cylinder into a spiral — this creates the layers.
5. Rest again ~10–15 minutes, then flatten each spiral into a thin disc.
6. Pan-fry in a little oil over medium heat until golden and puffed on both sides, tearing the layers with your hands to make it flaky.
7. Optionally fry an egg on top, lay in lettuce, ham, and a cheese slice, and fold or roll it up. Serve hot.

### 2. 茶叶蛋 (Tea Eggs) — Companion
**Difficulty:** ★★★ (3/5) | **Category:** 早餐 (Breakfast) | **Prep:** ~30 minutes, make-ahead friendly

**Ingredients (for ~4 people, scale up):**
- Eggs — 400 g (about 8 eggs)
- Star anise (八角) — 4 g (~2 pieces)
- Bay leaves (香叶) — 0.5–1 g (~2 leaves)
- Cinnamon (桂皮) — 3 g (1 small piece)
- Fennel seeds (茴香) — 5 g
- Rock sugar (冰糖) — 15 g
- Black tea (红茶) — 20 g
- Light soy sauce (生抽) — 15 g
- Dark soy sauce (老抽) — 25 g
- Salt (食盐) — 3 g

**Method:**
1. Hard-boil about 8 eggs, crack the shells lightly across the surface.
2. In a pot, combine water, tea, rock sugar, and all the spices.
3. Add the eggs, bring to a boil, then reduce to low heat and simmer 20–30 minutes.
4. Let the eggs soak in the broth overnight for maximum flavor — perfect for a weekend brunch.
